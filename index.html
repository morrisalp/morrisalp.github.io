Morris Alper

I am an Assistant Professor at the University of Miami in the Department of Computer Science. My research explores the intersection of multimodal machine learning and linguistics. I am especially interested in applications to digital humanities and low-resource languages, spanning textual, visual, and audio modalities. If you are interested in working with me, please reach out.

I completed my PhD in multimodal machine learning at Tel Aviv University. My BSc was from MIT, with a dual major in mathematics and linguistics.
